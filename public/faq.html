Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We have answers. If you do not see what you are looking for, give us a call.

Are you insured and bonded?
Yes. Kenest Cleaning Co. is fully insured and bonded. We carry general liability insurance to protect your property throughout every clean.
Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?
Yes. We bring all cleaning supplies and professional-grade equipment to every job. If you have a preferred product for specific surfaces, just let us know and we will accommodate when possible.
Do you require long-term contracts?
No. We offer month-to-month service. You are never locked into a contract. We earn your continued business through consistent quality and reliable service.
Can you clean after business hours or on weekends?
Yes. We offer flexible scheduling including evenings and weekends to minimize disruption to your operations. We work around your schedule.
How do you price commercial cleaning?
Pricing is based on square footage, frequency, and the services included. We do not use a one-size-fits-all rate. We offer a free walkthrough and provide a custom quote with no obligation.
What areas do you serve?
We serve St. Louis City, St. Louis County, and St. Charles County. If you are outside these areas, give us a call — we may still be able to help depending on the job.
What if I am not satisfied with the clean?
We stand behind our work. If you are not satisfied, contact us within 24 hours and we will return to fix it at no additional charge. Your satisfaction is not optional for us — it is the standard.

Still have questions?

Reach out and we will get back to you quickly.
