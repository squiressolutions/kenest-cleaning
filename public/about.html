Our Story

Built for St. Louis

Kenest Cleaning Co. is a St. Louis-based cleaning company built on reliability, attention to detail, and clear communication. We serve local businesses, builders, and homeowners with cleaning plans built around their schedule.

We started with a simple belief: cleaning should be done right, every time, without excuses. Our team shows up prepared, works efficiently, and treats every space with the same care we would want for our own.

As a locally owned company, we are invested in the St. Louis community. When you hire Kenest, you are supporting a local business that is committed to delivering real value — not just checking a box.
